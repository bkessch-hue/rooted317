ROOTED317

Our Purpose

Seven expressions of one calling. We exist to worship God, proclaim the Gospel, build disciples, strengthen believers, serve others, and represent Christ in the world.

Rooted in Christ. Transformed by His presence. Empowered to impact the world.

ROOTED

01

Worship and Glorify God

Created to worship.

We exist to worship God in spirit and truth and to declare His glory among every nation and every generation.

“But the hour is coming, and now is, when the true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and truth.”

John 4:23

Declare His glory among the nations. Psalm 96:3

✦

02

Preach the Gospel and Make Disciples

Sent into all the world.

Jesus commissioned His Church to proclaim salvation, baptize believers, and develop faithful disciples of every nation.

“Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit.”

Matthew 28:19–20

Go into all the world and preach the Gospel. Mark 16:15

◉

03

Equip and Build Up Believers

Growing strong spiritual roots.

We help believers grow through biblical teaching, discipleship, encouragement, correction, prayer, and meaningful fellowship.

“For the equipping of the saints for the work of ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ.”

Ephesians 4:11–12

Stir one another toward love and good works. Hebrews 10:24–25

↟

04

Fellowship and Unity

Many members. One body.

We cultivate a Christ-centered community where believers pray, learn, worship, serve, and grow together in unity.

“They devoted themselves to the apostles’ teaching and fellowship, to the breaking of bread and to prayer.”

Acts 2:42

Though many, we are one body in Christ. Romans 12:5

∞

05

Serve and Care for Others

Love made visible.

We demonstrate the love of Jesus through compassion, generosity, practical service, and care for people in need.

“By this all people will know that you are My disciples, if you have love for one another.”

John 13:35

Care for those experiencing hardship. James 1:27

♥

06

Be the Body of Christ on Earth

His hands. His feet. His light.

We represent Jesus by reflecting His truth, grace, mercy, holiness, and love throughout our communities.

“Now you are the body of Christ, and each one of you is a part of it.”

1 Corinthians 12:27

You are the light of the world. Matthew 5:14

✝

07

Prepare for Christ’s Return

Living with eternal hope.

We live faithfully, prayerfully, and expectantly as we prepare for the glorious return of Jesus Christ.

“Looking for the blessed hope and glorious appearing of our great God and Savior Jesus Christ.”

Titus 2:13
✧

The Vision of ROOTED317

Deeply rooted. Spiritually transformed.

To see people deeply rooted in Christ, transformed by His presence, and empowered to impact their communities with the Gospel.

To see a vibrant generation of young adults who are spiritually transformed, passionately serving Christ, and impacting their communities with the love of God.

“That Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith; that you, being rooted and grounded in love...”

Ephesians 3:17
